Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on electrical load sanctioning, DISCOM approvals, commercial revenue split, and technical compatibility.

What power sanction / electrical load is required for an EV charging station?
For residential AC smart wallboxes, a standard 7.4kW single-phase or 11/22kW three-phase LT connection is sufficient. For commercial DC Fast Charging stations (60kW to 120kW), a dedicated LT or HT commercial EV connection from your local electricity DISCOM (such as BSES, Tata Power, MSEDCL, BESCOM, UGVCL) is required. VILTRON handles the entire load application, transformer sizing, and electrical inspectorate approval on your behalf.
Can any electric vehicle in India charge at a VILTRON station?
Yes. VILTRON chargers strictly comply with ARAI and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications. Our AC chargers feature the universal Type-2 standard (compatible with Tata Nexon EV, Tiago, Punch EV, MG ZS EV, Mahindra XUV400, Hyundai, BYD, BMW, Mercedes). Our DC Fast Chargers feature CCS-2 (Combined Charging System 2), the prevailing high-speed DC standard across all 4-wheeler EVs in India.
How does billing, UPI payment, and revenue sharing work for hosts?
Drivers scan a dynamic UPI QR code on the charger or use the VILTRON / Bharat EV roaming app. Once charging is complete, funds are automatically processed. Property hosts get 24/7 access to our cloud CMS dashboard to view every session, kWh dispensed, and net earnings. Revenue settlements are deposited directly into your company bank account on a bi-weekly or monthly schedule.
How long does the turnkey installation process take?
For standard AC wallboxes, installation and commissioning is completed within 24 to 48 hours. For commercial and highway DC Fast Charging stations, civil foundation work, HT panel cabling, DISCOM inspection, and cloud commissioning typically takes between 10 to 21 business days.
What warranty and after-sales support does VILTRON provide?
All VILTRON hardware comes with a 3-Year comprehensive manufacturer warranty (extendable to 5 years). Our centralized Network Operations Centre (NOC) monitors all chargers 24/7 via cellular 4G/Ethernet. We guarantee a 99.8% uptime commitment and a 4-hour on-site engineer response in metro cities.
